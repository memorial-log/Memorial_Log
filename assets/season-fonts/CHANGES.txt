Memorial Didone fixes optical size 96 for extreme display contrast.
Memorial Winter fixes optical size 24 and raises lowercase x-height by 15%, preserving baseline, descenders and upper ascenders, to follow the Winter reference.
Both families contain real static weights 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 and 900. All font-family/name records are renamed. These are derivatives, not original fonts drawn from scratch.
Other glyphs, OpenType layout and license metadata are retained.
